# Meet Our Bobotics Truck

We built our **Bobotics** robot by adapting a small remote-controlled truck. It combines a Raspberry Pi, distance sensors and a motor controller with a custom design that gives it its own identity.

## Hardware

These are the main components we used:

| Component | What we use it for |
|---|---|
| [Raspberry Pi 4 Model B — 8 GB RAM](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B09TTKT94J) | Runs the robot’s code, processes camera images and controls its behaviour. |
| [Amazon Basics 64 GB microSD card](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B08TJTB8XS) | Stores the operating system, Python code and audio recordings. |
| [10,000 mAh power bank](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0DCBFS9Y3) | Provides portable power for the onboard electronics. The linked INIU model is our product reference. |
| [Two TF-Luna distance sensors](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B087QWT52L) | Measure distances to help the robot detect nearby objects. |
| [L298N motor controller](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0CNT6KKZ6) | Connects the Raspberry Pi’s control signals to the driving and steering motors. |
| [Start/stop push button](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0H82FZF42) | Gives us a simple way to start and stop the robot. |
| [DUKUSEEK remote-controlled truck](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0FHKSJK4Q) | Provides the truck body and chassis that we adapted for the project. |

## Giving the Truck Its Own Look

We wanted the truck to look like a finished project, not just a collection of electronics mounted on a vehicle. We chose a red, white and charcoal colour scheme, with our crowned robot logo and UK flags tying the design together.

### The Front Flag

For the windscreen, we used a Union Jack image and printed it on glossy photo paper. This gives the front of the truck a bright, clean finish. The graphic can be printed at a local photo shop or a shop that produces stickers.

### The Red Cargo Cover

At the back, we designed a red cloth canopy featuring our Bobotics logo, “Autonomous Vehicle” lettering and UK flags. A subtle circuit pattern in the background connects the design to the technology inside.

The canopy sits over a small frame made from beams and is secured with screws. There is no solid cargo box underneath—the frame supports the cover and keeps its shape. The vehicle photos show how we want the finished design to look.

## Cover Dimensions

We sized the artwork to fit the rear frame:

| Panel | Finished size | Number needed |
|---|---|---:|
| Top | 13 cm wide × 8.5 cm deep | 1 |
| Long sides | 13 cm wide × 6.5 cm tall | 2 |
| Front and rear ends | 8.5 cm wide × 6.5 cm tall | 2 |

The print layout also includes a 5 mm allowance around the edges for joining and attaching the cover. We adjusted the artwork orientation so that the logos and lettering read correctly when the panels fold down around the frame.
