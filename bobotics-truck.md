# Our Bobotics Truck

We turned a small remote‑controlled truck into our Bobotics robot — a compact autonomous vehicle powered by a Raspberry Pi, distance sensors and a motor controller. It blends practical engineering with a bit of personality, giving the truck its own identity.

## Hardware We Used

The robot is built from a set of key components:

- [Raspberry Pi 4 Model B — 8 GB RAM](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B09TTKT94J)  

  This is the robot’s brain. It runs our code, processes camera images and controls how the robot behaves.

- [Amazon Basics 64 GB microSD card](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B08TJTB8XS)  

  Stores the operating system, Python scripts and audio files.

- [10,000 mAh power bank](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0DCBFS9Y3)  

  Provides portable power for all onboard electronics.

- [Two TF‑Luna distance sensors](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B087QWT52L)  

  Measure distances so the robot can detect nearby objects and avoid obstacles.

- [L298N motor controller](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0CNT6KKZ6)  

  Connects the Raspberry Pi’s control signals to the steering and driving motors.

- [Start/stop push button](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0H82FZF42)  

  A simple way to turn the robot on and off.

- [DUKUSEEK remote‑controlled truck](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/B0FHKSJK4Q)  

  The base vehicle we adapted to hold all the electronics.

## Giving the Truck Its Own Look

We didn’t want the robot to look like a bunch of electronics strapped to a chassis. So we gave it a proper colour scheme — red, white and charcoal — along with our crowned robot logo and UK flags to tie everything together.

### The Front Flag

For the windscreen, we printed a Union Jack on glossy photo paper. It gives the truck a bright, clean front and can be printed at any photo or sticker shop.

### The Red Cargo Cover

The rear canopy is made from red cloth and features our Bobotics logo, “Autonomous Vehicle” text and UK flags. A subtle circuit pattern in the background hints at the technology inside.

The cover sits on a small frame made from beams and is secured with screws. There’s no solid cargo box underneath — the frame alone supports the cloth and keeps its shape.

## Cover Dimensions

To fit the frame properly, we sized each panel as follows:

| Panel          | Finished size | Number needed |

|----------------|---------------|--------------:|

| Top            | 13 cm × 8.5 cm | 1 |

| Long sides     | 13 cm × 6.5 cm | 2 |

| Front and back | 8.5 cm × 6.5 cm | 2 |

We added a 5 mm allowance around each edge so the panels can be joined neatly. The artwork is rotated so the logos and text appear the right way up once the panels fold into place.
